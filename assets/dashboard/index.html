How the capture works

A device joins the Wi-Fi. Its OS probes a URL like /generate_204 to test for internet. The board's DNS answers every lookup with its own address, so that probe hits the board and gets a 302 redirect — the phone shows the captive-portal popup. Browsing the site's domain resolves to the board too, served with its cert. Watch it all stream above.
